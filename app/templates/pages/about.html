Why I do this

Most of my career has been spent where things break: support desks, production incidents, late-night outages. That's where you learn what separates systems that work from systems that only look like they work: clear ownership, good documentation, monitoring, and people who understand the whole picture.

Small businesses rarely get that. They get a pile of subscriptions, a few clever workarounds, and one person who knows how it all fits together. I started Ron Systems AI to change that: honest advice, plain English, and systems that keep running after I hand them over.

How I work

  • I listen first and recommend second. Sometimes the answer is to keep what you have.
  • I don't resell software or take commissions.
  • Everything I build is documented and handed over, including code and access.
  • I use AI where it saves real time, and I'll tell you when it doesn't.
