Emails

When you email me, I receive your email address and whatever you write. I use them only to reply and to discuss working together, and I don't sell or share them. You can ask me to delete them at any time.

Booking a call

Booking uses Proton Calendar, a separate service run by Proton AG with its own privacy policy. The name and email address you enter there are shared with me so I can hold the call. The booking page opens on Proton's site and sets no cookies on this one.

Visitor statistics

This site uses Umami, self-hosted on my own server, to count visits and see which pages are useful. It sets no cookies, doesn't track you across other sites, and doesn't store personal information.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies: no analytics, advertising or social media cookies, and none of its own. That's why there's no cookie banner.

Videos

Videos on this site are YouTube embeds in privacy-enhanced mode. YouTube doesn't set cookies when the page loads, but once you press play, YouTube (Google) may store information on your device under its own privacy policy.
